## AI-Powered Legal Intelligence and Citizen Assistance Platform

An AI-powered legal document simplification platform that helps users understand complex legal documents in simple language.

## Overview

AI-Powered Legal Intelligence and Citizen Assistance Platform is designed to make legal text easier to read and understand. Users can upload legal documents such as agreements, contracts, or notices, and the system extracts the text, analyzes important clauses, detects risky or missing terms, and explains the content in a simpler format. It also supports multilingual translation and AI-based legal Q&A to improve accessibility.

## Features

* Legal document upload and text extraction
* OCR-based text recognition from scanned documents
* Clause identification and classification
* Risk analysis for important legal terms
* Missing clause detection
* Simplified explanation of legal content
* Multilingual support
* AI-powered question answering from documents
* Modern and responsive user interface

* `ai-models/` – NLP, OCR, and document processing modules
